Classical vs Modern Encryption — Encrypt, Decrypt & Attack Simulation

Caesar Cipher  WEAK

A classical substitution cipher that shifts each letter by a fixed number. Only 26 possible keys — trivially broken by brute-force.

Vigenère Cipher  MEDIUM

An improvement over Caesar that uses a keyword instead of a single shift. Each letter of the key applies a different shift, making simple brute-force harder — but still vulnerable to frequency analysis.

AES Cipher  STRONG

Advanced Encryption Standard — symmetric block cipher with 128-bit keys. Industry standard used in banking, TLS, and disk encryption.
